# Momentify

Status: active

White workspace, warm neutral surfaces, near-black text, quiet gray borders. Forest green is the single accent and positive color; muted red indicates losses and errors. No gradients or shadows.

Geist Sans for interface text, tabular numerals for financial values. Compact labels, understated headings, concise actions. Navigation is narrow and persistent on desktop and directly accessible on mobile.

Paper trading is always visibly identified. Unknown values use an em dash. Market provenance, timestamps, costs, and stale states remain explicit.
